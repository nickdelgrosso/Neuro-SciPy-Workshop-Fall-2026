# Workshop Code of Conduct

This  workshop is a shared learning space. Everyone should feel welcome to ask questions, make mistakes, and learn together, regardless of their background or programming experience. These expectations apply to participants and instructors in all workshop sessions, breakout rooms, chats, and shared materials.

## Be engaged

- Arrive on time, follow along, and make a good-faith effort to take part in exercises and discussions.
- In breakout rooms, introduce yourself, contribute to the task, and help your group stay focused. Take turns sharing ideas and working through the code so everyone has a chance to participate.
- Ask for help when you need it, and support others without taking over their work.
- Participation can be through voice, chat, or shared work. Be understanding of accessibility needs, technical difficulties, and necessary breaks.

## Be polite and respectful

- Listen to others, avoid interrupting, and give everyone space to speak.
- Be patient with different levels of experience. Questions and mistakes are part of learning.
- Offer constructive feedback about ideas and code. Avoid personal criticism, dismissive remarks, or jokes at someone else's expense.
- Harassment, discrimination, bullying, and intimidating behavior are not acceptable.
- Respect people's privacy. Ask permission before recording, taking screenshots of participants, or sharing their personal information or work outside the workshop.

## Address concerns

If something makes you uncomfortable or you notice behavior that goes against this code, contact the instructor or workshop organizers privately. You do not need to confront the person yourself.

If asked to change your behavior, listen and make the adjustment. Organizers may intervene and, for serious or repeated problems, ask someone to leave the session or workshop.

Thank you for helping make this a welcoming and productive workshop.
